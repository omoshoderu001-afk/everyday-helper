Help & Guides 📚

Welcome to Everyday Helper. These short guides show you how to use each tool, keep your information organised and avoid losing your saved entries.

Before you begin: Your entries are saved in your browser on this device. Use Back up on the main app to download a copy before clearing browser data or changing devices.

1. Budget Planner 💰

Use the Budget Planner to compare the money you have with your planned expenses.

  1. Give the budget a name, such as “October spending”.
  2. Enter the total money available.
  3. Enter your planned food, transport and other expenses.
  4. Select Calculate budget to see your expenses and remaining amount.
  5. Select Save budget if you want to keep it in the app.

Example: If you have ₦50,000 and your listed expenses total ₦35,000, the planner shows ₦15,000 remaining. Remember to include regular bills and small purchases when estimating costs.

This tool is for personal planning and is not financial advice.

2. Daily Task Planner ✅

Turn a busy day into a clear list of actions.

  1. Enter one task, such as “Read Chemistry notes”.
  2. Add a due date if it helps you plan.
  3. Choose a priority and whether the task repeats daily or weekly.
  4. Select Add task, then tick it when complete.

Tip: Break a large task into smaller actions. “Study” is broad; “revise chemical bonding for 20 minutes” is easier to start.

3. Smart Shopping List 🛒

Plan purchases before you get to the shop.

  1. Add the item name and quantity.
  2. Enter an estimated unit price if you know it.
  3. Tick items as you buy them.
  4. Review the estimated total for items with prices entered.

Tip: Add essentials first, then compare your list with the amount you can spend. Prices may change, so treat estimates as a guide.

4. Savings Goal Tracker 🎯

Give a savings plan a clear target and track progress over time.

  1. Enter a goal name, target amount and the amount already saved.
  2. Create the goal.
  3. Whenever you save more, enter the amount in that goal and select Add savings.
  4. Use the progress bar and remaining amount to check how close you are.

Tip: Choose a realistic target and decide how often you will review your progress.

5. Quick Notes 📝

Keep simple reminders, ideas and lists in one place.

  1. Add a short title and write the note.
  2. Save it, then use search to find a note by words in its title or content.
  3. Use Copy note when you want to paste the text elsewhere.

Privacy tip: Avoid storing passwords, bank details or other highly sensitive information in notes.

6. Unit Converter 🔄

Convert common measurements supported by the app.

  1. Choose a category: weight, distance or temperature.
  2. Enter the number you want to convert.
  3. Choose the conversion direction.
  4. Select Convert to see the result.

For example, you can convert kilometres to miles, kilograms to pounds, or Celsius to Fahrenheit.

Saving, backups and device changes 🔐

  • Your entries are stored in this browser on this device. They are not automatically synced to another phone or computer.
  • Use Back up to download a file containing your saved app data. Keep it somewhere safe.
  • Use Restore to load a backup. Restoring replaces the data currently saved in the app, so check that you have the right backup first.
  • Clearing browser data, changing browsers or losing access to the device may remove your entries. Back up first.

Read the Privacy Policy for more information about how the website handles information.

Frequently asked questions

Does Everyday Helper need an account?

No Everyday Helper account is required for these tools. Your entries are stored locally in your browser.

Will my notes and plans appear on another device?

No, not automatically. Download a backup and transfer it securely if you want to move your data.

What if my saved entries disappear?

If you have a recent backup, use the Restore option. Restoring replaces current app data, so save a backup of the current data first if you need to keep it.

Can I use Everyday Helper on a phone?

Yes. Open the website in a supported mobile browser. Depending on the browser, you may be able to install it from the browser menu or use an install option shown by the site.

How do I report a problem or suggest an improvement?
