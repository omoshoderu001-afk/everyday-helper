About Everyday Helper 🌱

Everyday Helper is a simple toolkit designed to make everyday planning easier. It brings practical tools together in one place, so you can organise tasks, plan a budget, prepare a shopping list, track savings, keep quick notes and convert common units.

Our goal

Our goal is to make useful everyday tools easy to access on a computer or phone, with a clear and friendly experience.

Tools you can use

  • Budget Planner: estimate income, expenses and what remains.
  • Daily Task Planner: organise tasks and due dates.
  • Smart Shopping List: keep track of items and estimated costs.
  • Savings Goal Tracker: follow progress toward a savings target.
  • Quick Notes: save reminders and ideas.
  • Unit Converter: convert supported units.

Your information

The planning information you enter is stored in your browser on your device. It is not sent to an Everyday Helper account or synchronised automatically between devices. Use the app’s backup feature if you want to keep a copy or move your data.

Everyday Helper is a general-purpose organisation tool. Its budget and savings features are for personal planning and are not financial advice.
