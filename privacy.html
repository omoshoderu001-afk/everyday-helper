Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Everyday Helper is designed to work primarily in your browser. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the website.

Information you enter

Budgets, tasks, shopping lists, savings goals and notes are stored locally in your browser using device storage. The site does not send these entries to an Everyday Helper account or provide automatic cloud synchronisation. Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access locally stored information, so avoid entering highly sensitive details.

Backups and device changes

If you use the backup feature, the app creates a file for you to download. You control where that file is stored and who can access it. Deleting browser data may remove saved entries. Keep a backup if you need one.

Basic technical information

Your hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs as part of delivering the website. See GitHub’s own privacy information for details about its services.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

At the time of this policy, Everyday Helper does not intentionally use third-party advertising cookies or analytics tools in the app code. If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, this policy should be updated to explain the providers used, what data they may collect, and how users can manage relevant choices. Third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Children

Everyday Helper is a general-purpose tool and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features or services change. The date at the top will be revised when meaningful changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact details on the Contact page.

This is a plain-language privacy notice, not legal advice. Review it if you add third-party services or expand how information is collected.
