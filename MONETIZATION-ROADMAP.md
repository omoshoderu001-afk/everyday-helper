# Everyday Helper: realistic monetisation roadmap

## 1. Make it reliable first
- Test every tool on desktop and mobile.
- Keep the privacy note and data-export option visible.
- Fix bugs before adding ads or payments.

## 2. Learn what people return to use
- Share the link with a small group and ask what they actually use.
- Ask permission before collecting feedback.
- If you later add analytics, choose a privacy-respecting setup and disclose it in a privacy notice.

## 3. Possible ways to earn
### Advertising
Google AdSense may be an option for a website, subject to its current eligibility, policy and review requirements. Apply only when the site is ready. Never click your own ads or ask others to click them. Ads do not guarantee income.

### Premium features
Potential extras include advanced budget reports, extra export formats, custom categories, or optional cross-device sync. Many paid features require a payment provider and secure server-side logic. Never store payment details in browser storage.

### Partnerships
Later, explore relevant and clearly disclosed partnerships. Avoid misleading financial promises.

## Important limits
- Publishing a site does not automatically generate income.
- Revenue depends on real usage, audience, advertiser demand, compliance and other factors.
- Local browser storage is not cloud storage and does not sync between devices.
- Before adding analytics, ads, accounts or payments, publish an accurate privacy notice and review applicable platform rules and local requirements.
